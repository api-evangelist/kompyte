# Kompyte

> Kompyte is a competitive intelligence and sales enablement platform, part of Semrush. It
> automatically tracks competitors across websites, pricing pages, product releases, content, ads,
> social channels and review sites, scores what changed, and turns it into AI-generated daily
> digests and continuously updated sales battlecards for revenue teams. Kompyte publishes no public
> developer API, developer portal or API documentation as of 2026-07-19.

## Product

- [Kompyte](https://www.kompyte.com/): Competitive intelligence platform homepage.
- [Integrations](https://www.kompyte.com/integrations): Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Highspot, Showpad, Trello, Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive. No public API or webhooks are documented.
